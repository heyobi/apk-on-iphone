# Contributing to LiquidAPK

Thank you for helping. LiquidAPK, and LxTerm (which shares `core/`), is distributed through Apple's App Store as well as from
this repository, so contributions are accepted on these terms:

1. **License.** By submitting a contribution (a pull request, a patch, an issue with
   code, or any code, text or artwork), you license it under the GNU General Public
   License, version 3 or later (`LICENSE`), **together with the additional
   permission for app stores in `NOTICE.md`** (GPL-3.0 section 7). This lets your
   work ship in the App Store build exactly as it ships here; the source stays free
   for everyone under the GPL.
2. **Your work.** You confirm that you wrote the contribution, or otherwise have the
   right to submit it on these terms, and that it contains no code copied from
   projects under other licenses unless that is noted in `NOTICE.md` and their license
   allows it.
3. **Sign-off.** Add a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` line to each commit
   (`git commit -s`). It certifies the [Developer Certificate of Origin
   1.1](https://developercertificate.org/) and your agreement to points 1 and 2.

Contributions that cannot be accepted on these terms cannot be merged.
